# KenyalangDev — Get Involved

There are two main ways to be part of KenyalangDev.

## 1. Volunteer

Roll up your sleeves and help make the magic happen — meetups, workshops, the works.
Volunteers are the people who make our events run, and you can pitch in wherever your
skills fit.

### Coordinator Roles

Volunteers help across a few areas:

- **Designer** — branding, event visuals, social media graphics
- **Content Creator** — copywriting, blog posts, captions
- **Social Media Manager** — posting, community engagement, content calendar
- **Program Manager** — event planning, speaker coordination, logistics
- **Tech** — website, tools integration, technical event support
- **Finance** — budgeting, expense tracking
- **Sales** — sponsorships, partnerships

You don't need to commit to a specific role — you can also just say "match me to
wherever I'm needed."

## 2. Subscribe

Not ready to volunteer? Just join our calendar to keep up with what's happening.
Subscribe on **Luma** (luma.com/KenyalangDev) to get notified about upcoming events.
