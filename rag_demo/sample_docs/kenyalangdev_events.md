# KenyalangDev — Events & Activities

KenyalangDev brings the Sarawak tech community together through a mix of regular and
one-off events. The goal is always the same: learn things, build things, and have a
good time doing it.

## What We Run

- **Meetups** — casual gatherings to share what we're working on and meet other
  people in the local tech scene.
- **Workshops** — hands-on sessions where you actually build something, often
  beginner-friendly.
- **Hack nights** — evenings set aside to work on projects together.
- **Occasional parties** — because community is also about celebrating.
- **"Made in Sarawak" initiatives** — projects that showcase and support what the
  local community is building.

## Stay in the Loop

All of our events are listed on our **Luma calendar** at **luma.com/KenyalangDev**.
Subscribe there to get notified whenever a new meetup, workshop, or hack night is
announced. You can also follow **@kenyalangdev** on Instagram for updates.
