# KenyalangDev — About

KenyalangDev is **Sarawak's hub for tech enthusiasts** — a community for developers
and technology lovers who are into coding and building apps. We are on a mission to
**turn Sarawak into a thriving tech playground**.

Made with ❤️ in **Kuching, Sarawak**.

## What We're About

Three things guide everything we do:

- **Creative chaos** — we'd rather experiment and ship something fun than wait for
  the perfect plan.
- **Inclusive community** — everyone is welcome, whether you're a seasoned engineer
  or writing your first line of code.
- **Good times** — our events are meant to be genuinely enjoyable, not just another
  thing on your calendar.

## The Team

KenyalangDev is run by six software engineers: **Mizan, Melvin, Angel, Betsy, Izzat,
and Haziq**. Each of them is a Software Engineer who helps organise the community's
events and initiatives on a volunteer basis.
